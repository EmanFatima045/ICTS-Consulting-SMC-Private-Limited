Corporate Identity

ICTS Consulting (SMC-Private) Limited

Plan Better. Manage Smarter. Deliver Successfully.

ICTS Consulting (SMC-Private) Limited is a registered corporate consulting, technological, and language education enterprise based in Lahore, Pakistan. We bring together People, Knowledge, Technology, and Opportunities through four mutually reinforcing verticals:

•
Project Management: Feasibility analysis, EVM monitoring, Primavera P6/MS Project baselining, and PMO establishment.
•
ICT & Digital Solutions: Full-stack web portals, SaaS systems, cloud architectures, cybersecurity, and digital automation.
•
Foreign Languages & Mandarin Training: Official HSK 1–6, YCT for Young Learners, and BCT Business Chinese with certified mock examinations.
•
Business Promotion Consultancy: China-Pakistan trade matchmaking, supplier verification, and institutional partner development.
